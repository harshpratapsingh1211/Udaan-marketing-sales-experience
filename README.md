# Udaan India — Marketing & Sales Experience

## Overview

A public-safe case study summarising my Marketing & Sales internship experience at Udaan India, with a focus on B2B lead management, CRM implementation, database segmentation, marketing communication and commercial execution.

## Business Context

The internship involved B2B business development across three broad customer groups:

- Travel Agents
- MICE Companies
- Corporate Clients

A key operational challenge was maintaining consistent follow-up across a large B2B lead base.

## My Role

As a Marketing & Sales Intern, my work included:

- Supporting B2B lead management
- Proposing and implementing CRM processes
- Segmenting B2B contact data
- Supporting targeted marketing communication
- Preparing commercial and pricing documentation
- Coordinating with clients and internal teams

## Approach

### 1. Identify the Process Gap

Reviewed the existing lead-follow-up process and identified inconsistency in follow-up tracking.

### 2. Implement CRM

Proposed and implemented HubSpot CRM to improve lead visibility and follow-up tracking across key B2B segments.

### 3. Segment the Database

Worked with approximately 39,000 B2B contacts and classified them into:

- Travel Agents
- MICE Companies
- Corporate Clients

The classification used a rule-based approach using keywords and email-domain patterns in Excel.

### 4. Improve Marketing Communication

Supported AI-assisted outbound emails and marketing collateral tailored to different B2B audiences.

### 5. Support Commercial Execution

Contributed to pricing documentation and commercial proposals for a key corporate account.

## Outcomes

The work contributed to measurable improvements during the internship:

- **Lead follow-up compliance:** approximately 35% → 78%
- **Average lead response time:** approximately 30% estimated reduction
- **B2B database segmented:** approximately 39,000 contacts
- **Tracked business revenue contribution:** approximately ₹5 lakh

## Key Learning

The experience showed me that marketing execution and business development are closely connected.

Effective segmentation is valuable only when it improves what happens next — such as follow-up, communication, prioritisation and commercial action.

**Segmentation → Process → Communication → Commercial Outcome**
